Why you’re seeing this

30,000 subscription apps taught us what moves conversion. Here’s what we’d test first at Duomo.

Adapty runs subscriptions for more than 30,000 apps, so we see which onboarding and paywall changes lift revenue across the whole market, not just inside one app.

We pointed that at Duomo. We took your own screens, copy and prices from your live iOS flow and App Store listing, and two of your Meta ads, and rebuilt the path from the ad to the paywall around the five ideas with the most potential.

Your onboarding has changed since our October audit: a new welcome, eleven questions and a weekly trial. So this one starts from your live flow of 3 November and carries over the ideas that still apply.

Your ads × your flow

Keep the promise the ad made. Two of your Meta ads, two first screens.

Meta is where we see almost all of your ads. Today every install meets the same welcome, “Find daily peace and grow closer to God”, whichever ad brought it.

Flow Builder can open a different flow by where the install came from, so the first screen repeats what the ad said, and each ad is judged on the subscriptions it brings, not the installs.

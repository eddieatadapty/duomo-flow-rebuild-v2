# Duomo Growth Audit

**Live → https://eddieatadapty.github.io/duomo-flow-rebuild-v2/**

Five ideas to turn more Duomo users into subscribers, each built into a clickable version of Duomo's own onboarding. Prepared by Adapty.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Use the answers you collect | +10–20% CR to purchase |
| 02 | Say what the $9.99 buys, and show the year | +10–15% ARPU, from a bigger annual share |
| 03 | Ask for reminders after the purchase | +10–15% CR to purchase |
| 04 | Say exactly what happens after the free days | +3–8% CR to purchase, and fewer refund requests |
| 05 | Catch the people who close the paywall | +3–8% CR to purchase |

Every price, rating and feature comes from Duomo's App Store listing, website or in-app screens. Impact ranges are Adapty’s expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across 30,000+ subscription apps, not measured lift for Duomo. They overlap, so they don’t add up. Opening the flow on the ad’s promise carries no range: only a test per ad can give one.

Static single page, no build step: `python3 -m http.server`.
